# Archive: content kept for later phases

Nothing in this folder is part of the app or the build. It is kept so that useful work is not lost.

What is here:
- data/courses.legacy.ts: the original 40 lesson summaries (five courses, eight modules each). Much of this is advanced material that fits the future Builder and Researcher levels (GIS, Python, machine learning, Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs, UTM, advanced astronomy, space weather, satellite engineering).
- data/challenges.ts, projects.ts, experiments.ts, dataActivities.ts: the original challenges, projects, experiments and data activities.
- components/InteractiveSimulators.tsx: five simulators (orbit, spectral bands, solar wind, NDVI calculator, antenna array).
- types.legacy.ts: the original type definitions.

Rules before anything here is reused:
1. Every fact must be checked against a source. Several items were written by an AI tool and are unverified.
2. All sample numbers (for example NDVI values in data activities) were invented. Replace them with values calculated from real data.
3. All quiz questions must be rewritten. In the originals the correct answer is almost always the first and the longest option, and some wrong answers are jokes.
4. Do not reuse a simulator in a lesson unless it matches that lesson's topic.

Future content plan (not shown in the app):
- Explorer: the two courses in the app (Earth from Space, The Sun and the Night Sky).
- Builder: practical satellite, data and engineering work (CubeSats, ground stations, data activities).
- Researcher: Python, GIS, remote sensing, machine learning, scientific datasets and advanced physics.

Added in Phase 2:
- data/exploreTopics.draft.ts: the six Explore topic pages (Earth, Sun, Moon, Solar System, Stars, Space Exploration) from Phase 1. They were removed from the app because Course 2 now covers the Sun, Moon, planets and stars in plain language, and these pages are technical and unsourced. Rewrite and source them before reuse.

Phase 2.1: nothing was moved to the archive. The original Explore topic pages remain in data/exploreTopics.draft.ts.
